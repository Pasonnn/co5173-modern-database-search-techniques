4.2.1. Workload Stress-Test: Point Read-Heavy (Uniform vs. Skewed Zipfian, 5 Trials)
==========================================================================================
Workload Type                | B+ Tree Latency (µs)      | Dynamic ALEX Latency (µs)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Uniform Random (N=10,000)    |          1.00 ± 0.22 µs |          2.38 ± 0.54 µs
Skewed Zipfian (α=1.3)       |          0.65 ± 0.14 µs |          3.07 ± 0.56 µs
Insight: Dynamic ALEX leverages CDF model projection to navigate within bounded slots,
         while B+ Tree performs deterministic log_B(N) pointer node transitions.

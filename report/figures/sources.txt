1. rum_conjecture.svg
========================================================================================
- File: report/figures/rum_conjecture.svg
- Type: Authored Vector Diagram (Generated via scripts/generate_figures.py)
- Attribution: Authored by project team based on theoretical literature.
- Academic Foundation & Paper:
  Athanassoulis, M., Kester, M. S., Maas, L. M., Stoica, R., Idreos, S., Ailamaki, A., 
  & Callaghan, M. (2016). "Designing Access Methods: The RUM Conjecture." 
  Proceedings of the 19th International Conference on Extending Database Technology (EDBT), pp. 461–466.
- Description: Vector graphic illustrating the RUM trade-off triangle (Read, Update, and 
  Memory Amplification) with mapped positions for Apache Cassandra (LSM) and Elasticsearch (Inverted Index).

2. lsm_tree_architecture.svg
========================================================================================
- File: report/figures/lsm_tree_architecture.svg
- Type: Authored Vector Diagram (Generated via scripts/generate_figures.py)
- Attribution: Authored by project team synthesizing production storage engine designs.
- Academic Foundation & Papers:
  - O'Neil, P., Cheng, E., Gawlick, D., & O'Neil, E. (1996). "The Log-Structured Merge-Tree (LSM-Tree)." 
    Acta Informatica, 33(4), 351–385.
  - Apache Cassandra Storage Engine Architecture & RocksDB Architecture Documentation.
- Description: Schematizes the write ingestion pipeline through sequential CommitLog WAL and RAM MemTable, 
  spilling into immutable SSTables with in-memory Bloom filters, sparse fence indexes, and asynchronous compaction.
